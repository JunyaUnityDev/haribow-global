The First Solo Show

A leap that started on the street —
past the world,
and beyond.

There was no special environment. Just a park, a street, and practice — piled up until team HARIBOW reached the top of the world. This is their first solo show: world-class performance, and the stories of every member who built HARIBOW, condensed into a single night.

One Night Only

One night. This lineup.

The world's best, gathered for a single night. Every performer on this stage runs at the front line of the scene.

A selection of the program — the night includes further acts, an opening, a finale and a collaboration. Program subject to change.

GO BEYOND

The Story So Far

We've made the world roar.

Everything so far was a waypoint.
But “beyond” was never about going somewhere farther away.

All this time, we've simply been continuing that day in the park.

Since childhood — same friends, same game.
It was never talent. We just never let it stop.

— Come remember the “something” and the “someone” you once let go.
