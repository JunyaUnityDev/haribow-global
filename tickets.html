FAQ

When do I get the watch link?

Your personal viewing page is issued right after purchase. The actual stream URL appears on that page by Sep 28 (JST), and we also email it to you the day before the show.

What if I miss the live stream?

The archive is available at the same link until October 29. No extra steps.

Can we watch as a group?

Yes — one ticket, one screen, as many people around it as you like. Watch parties welcome.

My card was declined — what do I do?

Foreign-issued Visa / Mastercard / Amex / JCB are officially supported. If your card is declined, it's usually your bank flagging an overseas (Japan, JPY) charge — a quick note to your card company solves it. Still stuck? Email info@haribow.com and we'll sort you out personally.

Refunds?

No refunds after purchase, except if the stream fails on our side. If anything goes wrong during the show, a backup link appears on your viewing page automatically.
